Themes in dark and light: v14

New: a Places group with Rainier, a Pacific Northwest theme with the mountain and a few firs drawn in the bottom-right corner over misty grey-green. Monsoon is redrawn as a small rain cloud in rain-washed teal. Spring now has a flowering branch in leaf green and blossom pink, its one exception to the single-colour motif rule. Every theme still comes in a dark and a light version, with the same font, paper and motif in both. The light version is chosen automatically when the phone is in light mode, and you can switch between them by hand. In both versions, text keeps at least 7:1 contrast against the background.
