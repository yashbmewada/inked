Inked. v11: Clean, final direction

This follows the brand expert's call: the Clean direction, with glass used only on the Style sheet. Light mode follows the phone's setting. All controls share 12px corners, and the letter preview uses 8px.

"Inked looks like a good correspondence card: warm off-black or cream, one fine line, one light typeface, and a single red full stop."
